Khartoum: Where the Two Niles Meet

Khartoum is the capital of Sudan, it is where the White Nile meets the Blue Nile to become the world's longest river upon whose banks flourished the civilisations of the Nile Valley including that of Ancient Egypt and Kush. Even though it was founded formally as a military outpost during the Turco-Egyptian expedition, it was continously inhabitated including the ancient city of Soba which was the capital of the Kingdom of Alodia and which has now been subsumed into the city. As the capital, it is the center of government and commerce in the country.

The main attractions of Khartoum are::

  • The Republican Palace.
  • Corinthia Hotel
  • Alnilein Mosque
